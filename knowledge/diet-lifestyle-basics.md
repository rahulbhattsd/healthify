# Foundational Lifestyle and Dietary Guidance for Recovery

Everyday habits directly influence the body's resilience, recovery trajectory, and overall immune vigor. While medications address specific symptoms, lifestyle adjustments provide the physiological foundation necessary for natural recuperation.

## Hydration Essentials
Proper hydration is crucial for cellular repair, temperature regulation, and mucosal lining moisture:
- Base fluid goal: Aim for roughly 2 to 2.5 liters of fluid per day for adults, adjusting upwards during illness with fever, sweating, or gastrointestinal loss.
- Fluid selection: Pure water, herbal teas (such as chamomile, ginger, or peppermint), and diluted mineral-rich broths are optimal. Limit caffeinated, sugary, or alcoholic drinks, which can promote dehydration and disrupt circadian rhythms.

## Nutrition for Immune Defense and Healing
Nutrient-dense foods provide the micro- and macronutrients needed to synthesize immune cells and combat oxidative stress:
- Whole foods and colorful produce: Leafy greens, citrus fruits, berries, bell peppers, and cruciferous vegetables provide vitamin C, flavonoids, and antioxidants.
- Adequate protein: Protein is necessary to build antibodies and repair tissues. Incorporate lean meats, poultry, eggs, legumes, tofu, or Greek yogurt.
- Anti-inflammatory fats: Sources like extra virgin olive oil, avocados, and omega-3 fatty acids (found in salmon, chia seeds, and walnuts) help modulate systemic inflammatory responses.

## Rest, Sleep Hygiene, and Stress Reduction
Physiological repair surges during deep sleep phases:
- Prioritize 7 to 9 hours of uninterrupted sleep per night. Maintain a consistent bedtime and create a cool, dark, and screen-free sleeping environment.
- Rest versus exertion: During active acute illness, suspend intense workouts. Gentle stretching or brief, slow walks can improve circulation without depleting vital immune reserves.
- Stress mitigation: Chronic emotional or mental stress elevates cortisol, suppressing immune function. Incorporate breathing exercises, meditation, or quiet reading to promote parasympathetic nervous system tone.
