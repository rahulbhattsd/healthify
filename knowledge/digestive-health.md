# Managing Common Digestive Discomforts

Digestive issues such as heartburn, mild nausea, bloating, and transient diarrhea or constipation are common occurrences that frequently respond well to targeted self-care, dietary adaptations, and temporary OTC remedies.

## Heartburn and Acid Reflux
Gastroesophageal reflux occurs when acidic stomach contents travel back upward into the esophagus, producing a burning sensation behind the breastbone.
- Non-pharmacological measures: Eat smaller, more frequent meals rather than large feasts. Avoid lying down for at least 2 to 3 hours after eating. Elevate the head of the bed by 6 inches. Limit trigger foods like caffeine, chocolate, citrus, tomatoes, peppermint, alcohol, and spicy or greasy foods.
- OTC options: Antacids (calcium carbonate, magnesium hydroxide) provide rapid, short-term relief by neutralizing acid. H2-blockers (famotidine) or proton pump inhibitors (omeprazole) provide longer-lasting acid suppression for frequent symptoms.

## Mild Nausea, Upset Stomach, and Diarrhea
Gastroenteritis or dietary indiscretions can disrupt normal bowel function:
- Rehydration: When experiencing loose stools or vomiting, fluid and electrolyte replacement is the highest priority. Sip oral rehydration salts (ORS), dilute broths, or electrolyte waters in small, frequent amounts.
- Diet progression: Follow a gentle, bland diet such as the BRAT pattern (bananas, white rice, applesauce, toast) and avoid dairy, high-fat, or high-fiber foods until digestion stabilizes.
- OTC aids: Bismuth subsalicylate can soothe indigestion and reduce diarrhea frequency (avoid in children/teens or those on aspirin). Loperamide can slow gut motility in non-infectious, uncomplicated diarrhea.

## Digestive Warning Signs
Contact a doctor promptly if digestive issues include: severe or localized abdominal pain, involuntary weight loss, swallowing difficulties, yellowing of the skin or eyes (jaundice), inability to keep liquids down for more than 24 hours, or signs of gastrointestinal bleeding (black tarry stools or vomiting blood).
